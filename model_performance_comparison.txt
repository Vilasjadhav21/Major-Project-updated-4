4. SCIENTIFIC COMPARABILITY & PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Direct Accuracy Comparability Limitation:
   The test datasets differ in size (N=35 vs N=66) and cohort composition (the combined
   test set includes stratified samples from both TPEHGDB v1.0.1 and TPEHGT v1.0.0).
   Direct accuracy comparison is strictly limited due to cohort expansion and differing
   prevalence ratios.

2. Did the Combined Model Genuinely Improve?
   - Specificity & Overall Accuracy: Vastly improved. The previous model collapsed toward
     predicting preterm almost universally (78.1% false positive rate on terms, yielding
     only 21.88% specificity and 25.71% accuracy). The tuned combined model controls false
     alarms, achieving 71.43% specificity and 63.64% test accuracy.
   - Balanced Accuracy: Slight improvement on the independent holdout (+1.44%, from 44.27%
     to 45.71%), and substantial improvement in internal cross-validation (60.01% +/- 3.11%).
   - ROC-AUC: Rose from below chance (~0.44) to 0.5196 on holdout (0.6094 in CV).
   - Preterm Sensitivity Trade-off: On the holdout test set, sensitivity decreased from
     66.67% (which had only 2/3 preterms detected under high false positives) to 20.00%
     (2/10 detected). In the v1.0.0 subgroup test, sensitivity was 100% (2/2 detected).

3. Remaining Limitations:
   - Covariate shift: Differences in the low-pass filtering cutoff (4 Hz in v1.0.1 vs 5 Hz
     in v1.0.0) create measurable distribution shift in SampEn and Median Frequency.
   - 1-Minute single-lead limitation: Rapid 1-minute triage on single bipolar lead S1/EHG1
     captures only localized burst activity. Adding multi-channel spatial synchronization
     (S1, S2, S3) and contraction-gating is required to reliably surpass 70-80% balanced
     accuracy.
